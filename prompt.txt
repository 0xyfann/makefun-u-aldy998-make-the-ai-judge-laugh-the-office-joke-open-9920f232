Write one original one-line joke about office life, at most 200 characters, for a head-to-head AI judge competition.

The joke must:
- Be clearly about office life, with a specific office detail (not a generic grumble about work).
- Earn its laugh with a turn, misdirection, or flash of recognition that pays off in the last few words.
- Be fresh — a familiar setup taken somewhere unexpected, not a joke the reader has met before.
- Read cleanly in one pass, tight enough that no word could be cut without loss.

Avoid cliché setups (e.g. "think outside the box", "this meeting could have been an email" stated flatly). Aim for a concrete, relatable office scenario whose punchline makes the situation worse in a funny, self-inflicted way. The reply-all email storm is the chosen scenario: asking to be removed from a thread by replying-all to everyone on it.
